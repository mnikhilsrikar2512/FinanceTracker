FAQs

Common questions

I cannot sign in. What should I check first?

Confirm your email/password, then reset password if needed. If access is still blocked, contact support for account status verification.

Can you help us configure Finly for our team?

Yes. We guide category structure, budget setup, and reporting views so your team starts with a clean baseline.

What details should I share for a bug report?

Include page name, action performed, expected result, actual result, and timestamp for quick investigation.

Do you support report/export quality checks?

Yes. We can review CSV/PDF formatting expectations and align output with your reporting standards.
